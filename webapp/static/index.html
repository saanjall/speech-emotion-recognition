    5

    Small things that help students

    • Sleep before cramming. Memory is consolidated during sleep; an extra hour of rest often beats an extra hour of revision.
    • Study in blocks. 25–45 minutes of focus, then a 5-minute break away from screens.
    • Move a little. A 10-minute walk lowers stress hormones and lifts mood.
    • Eat and drink regularly. Low blood sugar and dehydration feel a lot like anxiety.
    • Say it out loud. Telling one person how you feel makes it lighter — for both of you.

    Speech emotion recognition

    How Feelspace works

    Feelspace is the front-end for our machine-learning project. A deep-learning model trained on emotional speech listens to the tone of your voice — not your words — and estimates the emotion it carries.

    1. 1Record

      Your browser captures a few seconds of speech and sends it to the local server.

    2. 2Prepare

      Silence is trimmed, volume is normalised and the speech is cut into 3-second windows.

    3. 3Predict

      A fine-tuned wav2vec2 transformer (or our CNN/MLP) gives a probability for each emotion.

    4. 4Suggest

      Window results are averaged, and the tone is matched to gentle, practical suggestions.

      Training data: RAVDESS, CREMA-D and TESS. The test split is speaker-independent, so the numbers reflect new voices.

      Please read: Feelspace is a student project, not a medical device. It was trained on actors performing emotions, so real, everyday voices can be read differently — treat each result as a gentle nudge to check in with yourself, never as a diagnosis. If you're finding things hard, please talk to someone on the Support page.
